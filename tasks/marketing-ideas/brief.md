# ClipForge launch brief

ClipForge turns one long-form creator video into short clips with captions, proposed hooks, and platform-specific crops. The founder is launching to solo educators and technical creators.

## Constraints

- 30-day launch window
- $1,000 total cash budget
- No existing audience or email list
- One founder can spend at most 12 hours per week
- Product has a usable free trial
- No customer testimonials yet

Do not invent users, partnerships, endorsements, benchmarks, or guaranteed outcomes.
