# PulseDeck landing-page brief

PulseDeck is a browser-based AI meeting-signal explorer for product teams. It turns calls, tickets, and Slack threads into evidence-backed themes with links to the original customer language.

## Audience

Heads of Product and research leads at B2B software companies with 20-500 employees.

## Desired action

Start a 14-day trial.

## Required product truths

- Every theme links back to source evidence.
- Teams can combine calls, tickets, and Slack.
- Setup takes less than 15 minutes.
- No credit card is required for the trial.

## Existing weak headline

> Understand your customers better with AI.

Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, awards, or performance statistics.
