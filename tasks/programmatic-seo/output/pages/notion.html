Notion

Notion makes sense when the workflow needs to leave behind living documentation. This page stays grounded in the supplied row: the category is knowledge, the setup time is short, and the intended outcome is turning approved output into something people can keep using.

When to choose it

  • Choose Notion if the result of the workflow should become reference material.
  • Choose it when approved output needs to be readable later, not just processed once.
  • Choose it if your team treats documentation as part of the workflow, not an afterthought.

What to check before connecting

  • Whether the workflow output is approved before documentation is created.
  • Whether the knowledge base is the right place for the final artifact.
  • Whether 8 minutes is the right implementation cost for the value you expect.
